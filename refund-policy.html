Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 27, 2026 | Razorpay Merchant Compliant

At Utkio, we provide digital spoken English learning services. This policy details our cancellation and refund framework.

1. Instant Digital Delivery

Upon successful payment via Razorpay, subscription benefits (unlimited scenarios, priority AI latency, Commit Mode status) are activated on your account instantly within 30 seconds. No physical shipping is required.

2. Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel recurring subscriptions anytime from your app profile or by emailing support@utkio.com. Benefits remain active until the end of the paid 30-day billing cycle.

3. Refund Guidelines

Eligible Cases: Duplicate payment charges or technical provisioning failures where premium features failed to activate within 24 hours.

Approved refunds will be credited back to the original payment source (UPI, Card, Bank) within 5 to 7 business days.
