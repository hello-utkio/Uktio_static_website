Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 27, 2026 | Effective Date: August 27, 2026

At Utkio ("we", "our", or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable digital privacy regulations.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Mobile phone number for OTP authentication, user name, and optional learning preferences.
  • Voice & Audio Data: Live microphone audio during practice conversations, processed in real time via secure Google Gemini Live WebSockets API.
  • Analytics & Practice Stats: Session duration, scenario completion scores, filler words analysis, and practice streak records.
  • Payment Details: Transaction IDs and plan subscription records processed via Razorpay. We never store credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide real-time interactive spoken English practice and instant speech feedback.
  • To track daily practice streaks and Commit Mode milestone achievements.
  • To send transactional OTP verification messages and critical service alerts.
🔒 Zero Public Machine Learning Training: We never sell your personal voice data, session transcripts, or phone numbers to third parties or public advertising brokers.

3. Device Permissions & Microphone Safety

Utkio requests Microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) solely during active voice calls initiated by the user. The microphone is turned off immediately when a call concludes.

4. Data Deletion & Contact

You may request account data deletion or ask privacy questions anytime by emailing support@utkio.com.
